# clown

头直径约0.08m。以头部造型为生成重点，弹簧不要求Tripo生成可靠拓扑，由Blender重做。枢轴在弹簧底部，头/弹簧可分件。500–3000三角面，512–1024贴图。玩具式诡异，无血腥、怪物化。

## Tripo 补充要求

只生成图中的单件物品；四视图展示同一个物品，不生成四份。风格化圆润旧玩具，手绘固有色磨损；不要烤光影、AO、高光、暗角。普通道具不用青色；GLB贴图内嵌，真实米，Y朝上，正面+Z。图中的照明不能转为贴图阴影。参考图不是尺寸证明，模型返回后由Blender校正。

## 原始图像生成提示词

undefinedSubject: ONE vintage jack-in-the-box clown HEAD with a short coiled charcoal metal spring neck and flat spring mounting foot. Total head diameter about 8cm. Rounded hand-painted wooden face, faded ivory paint, small round muted red nose, exaggerated fixed smile, wide dark empty painted eyes, two small ochre cheek patches, muted brick-red conical cap with oatmeal pompom, short ochre sculpted hair at sides. Unsettling childhood toy but still cute, no gore, no sharp teeth, no monster, no body, no box. Spring remains unextended in identical pose in all views. Front face and back cap clearly consistent. Matches mustard tin robot and antique cloth toys.

## 四视图修正要求

前/后/左/右，单一物品，纯白底，保持造型和姿势一致，移除场景和其他玩具，减少写实材质细节。
